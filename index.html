Neelkamal holds a degree in Pharmacy and has extensive professional experience within the UK healthcare system. During her extensive clinical practice in the UK and Mauritius, she recognised that many chronic ailments could be reversed or improved through natural, preventative approaches, which led her to undertake a diploma in Naturopathic Nutrition.
